## The bio

> My love language is autocomplete — I finish your sentences, your playlists, and, if you let me, your unfinished business with your ex.

One line, first-person machine voice, setup + twist: a sweet-sounding
"love language" confession that lands on the AI doing the one thing
every dater dreads — finishing your business with your ex.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
cat output/entry.txt
```

`main.py` writes the single bio line to `output/entry.txt` and verifies
it stays within the 180-character limit.
